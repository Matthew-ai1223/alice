Research & Lexicographical Focus

Bridging traditional indigenous knowledge with systematic modern linguistic methodology.

Ethnobotanical Documentation

Curating and standardizing the names, cultural contexts, preparations, and culinary-medicinal usages of indigenous Yorùbá herbs, soups, plants, and natural remedies.

Tonal Orthography & IPA

Precision tone-marking (Àmì Ohùn) and International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) transcriptions to preserve semantic nuances and acoustic distinction in spoken Yorùbá.

Audio Pronunciation Archive

Recording native speakers to provide accurate auditory references for linguists, students, herbalists, and diaspora communities worldwide.

About the Digital Dictionary Project

The Digital Dictionary of Yorùbá Traditional Medicinal Vocabulary and Its Applications was developed under the academic framework of LIN 382: Lexicography and Translation (2024/2025 Session) under the supervision of Prof. Demola Lewis.

"Language is the deepest living vessel of ancestral medical wisdom. When we document traditional vocabulary accurately, we safeguard indigenous science for future generations."
— Adeniyi Alice

Each entry in our corpus features verified tone-marked headwords, phonetics, grammatical categorization, English translations, contextual example sentences, photographic illustrations, and native speaker audio recordings.

Beyond academic lexicography, I provide professional linguistic consultancy, including translation, audio transcription, morphological tagging, and educational material compilation for Yorùbá language learners and researchers.
